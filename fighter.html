HOW TO PLAY

Move: ◀ ▶   Jump: JUMP   Crouch: DUCK

LP / HP = light / heavy punch  ·  LK / HK = light / heavy kick
BLOCK = hold to guard (or hold ◀ away from foe)

★ SPECIAL MOVES ★
FIREBALL: hold ▶ toward foe + press HP
UPPERCUT (Rising): hold ◀ away + press HP
SPIN KICK: hold DUCK + press HK

Keyboard (desktop test): P1 = A/D move, W jump, S duck, F/G punch, V/B kick, Shift block. P2 = ←/→, ↑, ↓, K/L punch, N/M kick, Enter block.
